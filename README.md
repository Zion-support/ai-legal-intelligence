# AI Legal Intelligence

> Contract review and legal research at machine speed, with human-grade citations.

AI legal intelligence — contract analysis, clause benchmarking, research and obligation tracking. Part of the **Zion AI App Network** — 550+ interlinked AI & IT tools by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com).

## Features
- Contract review with clause extraction, risk flags and redline suggestions
- Clause benchmarking against your playbook and market standards
- Obligation and renewal tracking across the contract portfolio
- Legal research with citation-verified answers
- Matter summarization and chronology building
- Integrations: DocuSign CLM, Ironclad, SharePoint, iManage

## Use Cases
- Review NDAs and MSAs in minutes instead of days
- Never miss a renewal, notice period or obligation
- Give sales self-serve fallback positions from the playbook
